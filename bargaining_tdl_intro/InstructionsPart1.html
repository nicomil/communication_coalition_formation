  • In this part of the experiment, you will be randomly assigned to a group of exactly three participants (you and two others).

  • Each participant will be randomly assigned a unique color label to ensure complete anonymity.

  • Each participant has the same three options to choose how divide the $ 12. Imagine you are the Green Participant, and you are interacting with a Purple and an Orange Participant. You have to choose to:

    • Equally split the $ 12 among all the participants of the group;

    • Equally split the $12 with the Purple Participant;

    • Equally split the $12 with the Orange Participant;

  • If at least two participants in the group agree on the same division, you and the participants of your group will all be paid accordingly. Otherwise, if no agreenment is reached, you and the paticipants of your group will earn $ 0.

    • Chat Instructions

  • Before choosing your option to divide the $ 12, you have maximum 3 minutes to communicate freely with the other participants.

  • Messages are private, and you will only see the messages sent by you, and received by the each of the other participants. You will not see the messages sent between the other two participants, and viceversa (each participant will only see messages from conversations they are directly involved in). In these messages you can explain to each of them what division you intend to choose, and what division you think they should choose. You can write different messages to the different participants.

  • Before proceeding with your decision on how to divide the $ 12, you will be asked to briefly communicate your intentions to the other participants using a set of pre-written messages. As with the messages of the chat, your intentions will only be visible to the specific participant they are addressed to.

  • After the chat period, before choosing your option on how to divide the $ 12, is required to send your intentions choosing one pre-written message, to each of the other two participants. The pre-written messages to choose your intentions are the following:

    • I wish to split the $ 12 equally with you only.
    • I wish to split the $ 12 equally with the other Participant only.
    • I wish to split the $ 12 equally with the two Participants.

    Please note: Revealing your identity to other participants, and using, discriminatory or offensive language, are strictly prohibited. Conversations will be analyzed after the end of the experimental session and before payments are finalized. If it is found that the above conditions have been violated, your payment for this experiment will be completely forfeited. We expect and appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a keeping this polite.

    Summary

    In summary, Part 1 will proceed as follows:

    1. Chat with each of the other participants of the group.
    2. After the chat, each participant of the group declares its intentions addressed to each of the other participants of the group.
    3. After declaring their intentions, each participant of the group makes their final decision on how they would like to split the $ 12.
    4. Once the final decision has been made, payments are determined. Payments will be communicated at the end of the experiment.
