  • In this part of the experiment, you and the other participants will be randomly matched in groups of three.

  • Each participant will be assigned a unique color label to ensure complete anonymity.

  • You and the other two members of the group can choose how each of you would like to divide $ 12 between the three of you.

    • Each Participant has the same three options to choose how divide the $ 12. Imagine you are interacting with a Purple and an Orange Participant. You can choose to:

  • Equally splits the $ 12 among all the group members ($ 4 to each Participant in the group)

  • Equally split the $12 with the Purple Participant ($6 to you and $6 to the Purple Participant, and $0 to the Orange Participant)

  • Equally split the $12 with the Orange Participant ($6 to you and $6 to the Orange Participant, and $0 to the Purple Participant)

  • If at least two participants in the group agree on the same division, you will all be paid accordingly.

  • If no agreement is reached, all the group members earn $ 0.

    • For Example:

    Imagine you are the GREEN Participant, and in your group there are the Orange and Purple Participants.

    • Dividing the $12 equally among all members:
      • If at least one other Participant also chooses this option, each of you earns $4.
        (Example: You and Orange choose to split among all; Purple chooses to split only with Orange. All three members earn $4.)
    • Dividing the $12 equally with only one member:
      • If you and that specific Participant both choose each other, you both earn $6.
        (Example: You and Orange choose to split only with each other; Purple chooses to split among all. You and Orange earn $6.)
    • No Agreement:
      • If no Participants choose the same option, everyone earns $0.
        (Example: You choose to split among all; Orange chooses to split only with you; Purple chooses to split only with Orange. Everyone earns $0.)

    Chat Instructions

  • Before choosing your option to divide the $ 12, you have maximum 3 minutes to communicate freely with the other participants.

  • Messages are private, and you will only see the messages sent by you, and received by the each of the other participants. You will not see the messages sent between the other two participants.

  • Before choosing your option you can write your messages, to each of the other two participants. In these messages you can explain to each of them what division you intend to choose, and what division you think they should choose. You can write different messages to the different participants.

  • Before proceeding with your decision on how to divide the $ 12, you will be asked to briefly communicate your intentions to the other participants using a set of pre-written messages. As with the messages of the chat, your intentions will only be visible to the specific participant they are addressed to.

  • After the chat period, before choosing your option on how to divide the $ 12, is required to send your intentions choosing one pre-written message, to each of the other two participants. The pre-written messages to choose your intentions are the following:

    • Message 1: I wish to split the $ 12 equally with you only.
    • Message 2: I wish to split the $ 12 equally with the other Participant only.
    • Message 3: I wish to split the $ 12 equally with the two Participants.

    Please note: Revealing your identity to other participants, and using, discriminatory or offensive language, are strictly prohibited. Conversations will be analyzed after the end of the experimental session and before payments are finalized. If it is found that the above conditions have been violated, your payment for this experiment will be completely forfeited. We expect and appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a keeping this polite.

    Summary

    In summary, Part 1 will proceed as follows:

    1. Chat with each of the other members of the group.
    2. After the chat, each member of the group declares its intentions addressed to each of the other members of the group.
    3. After declaring their intentions, each member of the group makes their final decision on how they would like to split the $ 12.
    4. Once the final decision has been made, payments are determined. Payments will be communicated at the end of the experiment.
