Welcome!

Thank you for taking part in this experiment!

In this experiment, you and the other participants will make a series of decisions that will directly affect your monetary rewards. Your earnings will depend on both your own choices and those made by the other participants. Similarly, your decisions will play a role in determining the earnings of others. All the data are anonymous, and we only ask for your honest responses. The experiment consists of: two parts, Part 1 and Part 2, in either you can earn between $0 and $6 in a group choice task; a final survey; and an additional question in a group choice task.

Please note: Your payment for this experiment will be the sum of: $3 as participation fee, and completing the final survey; plus your payoff for either Part 1 or Part 2 (only one will be randomly selected for the payment); plus a maximum of $3.90 with an additional question in a group choice task. If you do not make any decision within 5 minutes, the computer will remove you from the experiment, and you will not receive any payment.
