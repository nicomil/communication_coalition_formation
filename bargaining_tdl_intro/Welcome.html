Welcome!

Thank you for taking part in this experiment!

In this experiment, you and the other participants will make a series of decisions that will directly affect your monetary rewards. Your earnings will depend on both your own choices and those made by the other participants. Similarly, your decisions will play a role in determining the earnings of others. All the data collected in this study are anonymous. The experiment consists of Part 1, in which your group choices determine your earnings; an additional question in a group choice task; and a final survey.

Please note: Your payment for this experiment will be an amount as the sum of the participation fee and an additional question in a group choice task. Furthermore you will be paid a variable amount from your group choices in Part 1 and a possible bonus from the additional question in a group choice task. If you do not make any decision within 5 minutes, the computer will remove you from the experiment, and you will not receive any payment.
