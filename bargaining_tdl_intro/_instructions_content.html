    Chat Instructions

  • Before choosing which outcome you will vote for, you have maximum 10 minutes to communicate freely with the other participants in two separate chats.

  • In these messages you can explain to each of them what division you intend to vote for, and what division you think they should vote for, and why. You can write different messages to the different participants.

  • At the end of the chat period you will select pre-written messages to send to each of the other two participants saying which option you intend to vote for. You can select different messages for the different participants.
