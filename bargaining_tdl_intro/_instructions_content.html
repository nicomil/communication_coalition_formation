• In this part of the experiment, you will be randomly assigned to a group of three participants (you and two others).

  • Each participant will be randomly assigned a unique color label to ensure complete anonymity.

  • Each participant will decide whether to support one of the other two participants or support no one.

  • If two participants support each other, they will earn $3 each, and the third participant will earn $0.

    • Chat Instructions

  • Before choosing who you will support, you have maximum 10 minutes to communicate freely with the other participants in two separate chats.

  • In these messages you can explain to each of them who you intend to support, who you think they should support, and why. You can write different messages to the different participants.

  • At the end of the chat period you will select pre-written messages to send to each of the other two participants saying who you intend to support. You can select different messages for the different participants.
