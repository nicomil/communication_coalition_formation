• In this part of the experiment, you will be randomly assigned to a group of three participants (you and two others).

  • Each participant will be randomly assigned a unique color label to ensure complete anonymity.

  • Each participant has the same three options to choose how to split the $12. Each participant will choose one of three options for splitting the $12. Each participant will decide which option they prefer.

  • For Example: Imagine you are the Yellow Participant, and you are interacting with a Purple and an Orange Participant. Your options are:

    • Equally split the $12 with both the two Participants (everyone gets $3);

    • Equally split the $12 only with the Purple Participant (you get $6, Purple gets $6, Orange gets $0);

    • Equally split the $12 only with the Orange Participant (you get $6, Purple gets $0, Orange gets $6);

  • If at least two participants in the group agree on the same option, you and the participants of your group will all be paid accordingly. Otherwise, if no agreenment is reached, you and your group will earn $0.

    • Now imagine you are the Yellow Participant, and in your group there are the Orange and Purple Participants. The possible outcomes, are the folliwing:

    • Dividing the $12 equally among all members:
      • If you and another Participant both choose to split equally with both the two Participants, each participant of your group earns $3.
        (Example: You (Yellow) and the Orange participant both choose to split equally with both the two participants of the group; The Purple participant chooses to split only with the Orange participant. In this case, all three participants earn $3.)
    • Dividing the $12 equally with only one member:
      • If you and another Participant both choose to split equally only with each other, you both earn $6, and the other Participant earns $0.
        (Example: You (Yellow) and the Orange participant choose to split equally only with each other; The Purple participant chooses to split equally with both the two participants. You (Yellow) and the Orange participants earn $6, and the Purple participant earns $0.)
    • No Agreement:
      • If no Participants choose the same option, everyone earns $0.
        (Example: You (Yellow) choose to split equally with both the two participants; The Orange participant chooses to split equally only with you (Yellow); The Purple participant chooses to split equally only with the Orange participant. In this case, everyone earns $0.)

    Summary

    In summary, Part 1 will proceed as follows:

    1. Chat with each of the other participants of the group.
    2. Each participant of the group sends a final message to each of the other participants of the group.
    3. Each participant of the group makes their final decision on how they would like to split the $12.
    4. Payments are determined and will be communicated at the end of the experiment.
    5. Please note that if a participant drops out before sending their final message or making a final decision, random decisions will be made for them.
    6. Note that all chat will be with real people: no chatbots are used in this experiment.
