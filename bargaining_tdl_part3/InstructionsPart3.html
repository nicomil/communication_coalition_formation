Please make your decision for Part 2. Note that if you have not made your decision within 10 minutes, the experiment will end and you will not be paid.

Please note that in Part 2 of the experiment, each participant will be randomly matched with different group members from those they were matched with in Part 1

In this part of the experiment, you will be asked how you would like to divide $12 between yourself (SENDER) and other two participants (RECEIVERS).

You have two options to choose how to divide the $12 :

  • Option 1: Split equally the $12 with both the two participants ($4 to you and $4 to each of the RECEIVERS).
  • Option 2: Split equally the $12 with only one of the two participant ($6 to you, $6 to one RECEIVER, and $0 to the other RECEIVER).

  • In case you choose Option 2, which of the two RECEIVERS get $6 and which gets $0 will be chosen randomly by the computer.

  • After the experiment is over, in case Part 2 is paid out, the computer will randomly decide whether you are to be a SENDER or a RECEIVER from another participant.

  • If you are randomly selected to be a SENDER, you and your two RECEIVERS will be paid according to your decision.

  • If you are randomly selected to be a RECEIVER:
    • you will be paid according to the decision of a SENDER who will be different from the participants you interacted with in Part 1 and different from the other RECEIVERS for whom you are making a decision.
