Please make your decision for Part 2. Note that if you have not made your decision within 10 minutes, you will be excluded from the experiment.

  • In this part of the experiment, you will be randomly matched in a different group of three participants(you and two others) from those you were matched with in Part 1.

  • In this part of the experiment, you will be asked to act as a SENDER, while the other group members will be RECEIVERS.

  • You will choose one of three options:

    • A) Everyone gets $2;
    • B) You get $6, one Receiver gets $6, one Receiver gets $0;
    • C) You get $0, one Receiver gets $6, one Receiver gets $6;

  • If you choose option B, which of the two RECEIVERS gets $6 and which gets $0 will be chosen randomly by the computer.

  • Your decision will be strictly anonymous, and you will not interact with these Receivers again in this experiment.

  • After the experiment is over, in case Part 2 is paid out, the computer will randomly decide whether you are to be a SENDER or a RECEIVER from another participant.

  • If you are randomly selected to be a SENDER, you and your two RECEIVERS will be paid according to your decision.

  • If you are randomly selected to be a RECEIVER:
    • you will be paid according to the decision of a SENDER who will be different from the participants you interacted with in Part 1 and different from the RECEIVERS for whom you are making a decision.
